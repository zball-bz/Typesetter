<p data-pid="0" data-s="0" data-e="141">Typed symbols are their names (plan P3-24): <code class="tsr-mathsrc">$∑_(i=1)^n a_i ≤ b$</code> sums like <code class="tsr-mathsrc">$sum_(i=1)^n a_i &lt;= b$</code>, and the sum ends at the typed relation.</p>
<p class="tsr-mathblock" data-pid="1" data-s="143" data-e="176"><code class="tsr-mathsrc">$ ∑_(i=1)^n a_i ≤ ∫_0^1 f $</code></p>
<p data-pid="2" data-s="178" data-e="305">Negation is one rule: <code class="tsr-mathsrc">$a != b$</code>, <code class="tsr-mathsrc">$x !in A$</code>, <code class="tsr-mathsrc">$!exists y$</code>, <code class="tsr-mathsrc">$p !| q$</code>, <code class="tsr-mathsrc">$u !|| v$</code>, <code class="tsr-mathsrc">$a !&lt;= b$</code>; a factorial stays one: <code class="tsr-mathsrc">$n! = n (n-1)!$</code>.</p>
<p data-pid="3" data-s="307" data-e="411">Alphabets: <code class="tsr-mathsrc">$bb(R)$</code>, <code class="tsr-mathsrc">$NN$</code>, <code class="tsr-mathsrc">$bb(1)$</code>, <code class="tsr-mathsrc">$cal(A)$</code>, <code class="tsr-mathsrc">$frak(g)$</code>, <code class="tsr-mathsrc">$bold(v)$</code>, <code class="tsr-mathsrc">$sans(x)$</code>, <code class="tsr-mathsrc">$mono(x)$</code> and <code class="tsr-mathsrc">$italic(h)$</code>.</p>
<p data-pid="4" data-s="413" data-e="466">Only parentheses shed: <code class="tsr-mathsrc">$x^(a b)$</code>, <code class="tsr-mathsrc">$x^{a b}$</code>, <code class="tsr-mathsrc">$x^[a]$</code>.</p>
<p data-pid="5" data-s="468" data-e="675">Delimiters stretch whatever their spelling: <code class="tsr-mathsrc">$⟨x/y, z⟩$</code>, <code class="tsr-mathsrc">$langle x/y rangle$</code>, <code class="tsr-mathsrc">$lfloor x/2 rfloor$</code>; a bar alone in a group is its middle, <code class="tsr-mathsrc">${x | x &gt; 0}$</code> and <code class="tsr-mathsrc">$P(A | B)$</code>, while <code class="tsr-mathsrc">$|x| + ||v||$</code> stay as they are.</p>
<p data-pid="6" data-s="677" data-e="810">Spaces and styles: <code class="tsr-mathsrc">$a thin b med c thick d quad e wide f$</code>, <code class="tsr-mathsrc">$display(sum_i x_i)$</code> inline, <code class="tsr-mathsrc">$limits(lim)_(x -&gt; 0)$</code> and <code class="tsr-mathsrc">$class(rel, A) B$</code>.</p>
